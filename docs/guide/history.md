# History & provenance

## The pane

*Open thesaurus history* (command palette or the ribbon icon) opens a pane on the right: every command call, newest first, grouped by day, with the time, how many suggestions were found, the note it was typed in, and — once you inserted something — what you picked.

- **Click an entry** to run the lookup again, right under that entry. Click a suggestion there to insert it at the cursor of the active note. Click the entry again, or ×, to close it.
- **Filter box**: narrows the list as you type; `Enter` looks up whatever you typed, at the top of the pane.
- **This story**: shows only the calls made in the notes of the active note's story (see [The story](/reference/front-matter)). Disabled when the active note is not in one.
- Hover an entry for the trash icon to remove it; the trash icon in the header clears everything.

Re-running a lookup from the pane is not a new call and is not recorded. Looking the same word up again straight after the first time refreshes the entry instead of repeating it.

## Where did that word come from?

**In a note**, hover a word you inserted from the box. A box shows the term you looked up, when, and *Also in this note (n)*: every other place that word appears, with line numbers and snippets. Click one to jump. Words you never picked show nothing.

**In the pane**, hover an entry: `original → picked`, the note it was typed in, then *"picked" appears 3 times in 2 notes in Novel* — the notes of the story, the source note first, then by count, each with its lines. Click a line to open the note there.

The story is what limits the search — not the vault. *Search in* → *The whole vault* widens it.
